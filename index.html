Deaths on Colorado's Fourteeners (14er)

Colorado's Fourteeners attract hundreds of thousands of hikers each year. Yet there is no single, comprehensive public record of the people who have lost their lives on these peaks.

Deaths on Colorado's Fourteeners was created to document these fatalities and preserve the stories behind them—including falls, rockfall, avalanches, exposure, medical events, lightning, and other causes.
